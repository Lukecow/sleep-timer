# Installing Sleep Timer

Sleep Timer is free and open source. Because it isn't (yet) signed with a paid
Apple developer certificate, your Mac will ask you to confirm it the **first time**
you open it. This takes about a minute and you only do it once.

**Needs:** macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer. Works on Apple-chip and Intel Macs.

## 1. Install it

1. Download **Sleep Timer.dmg**.
2. Double-click it. A window opens showing **Sleep Timer** and an **Applications** folder.
3. Drag **Sleep Timer** onto **Applications**.
4. You can now eject the "Sleep Timer" disk in Finder's sidebar and delete the .dmg file.

## 2. Open it the first time

1. Open your **Applications** folder and double-click **Sleep Timer**.
2. macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software. Click **Done**
   (don't move it to the Bin).
3. Open **System Settings** (Apple menu  → System Settings…).
4. Click **Privacy & Security** in the sidebar, then scroll down to the **Security** section.
5. You'll see a line saying *"Sleep Timer" was blocked…*. Click **Open Anyway**.
6. Click **Open Anyway** again in the box that appears, then enter your Mac password
   (or use Touch ID).

That's it. A **moon** appears at the top-right of your screen, in the menu bar.
From now on it opens normally, with no more warnings.

## 3. Use it

1. Click the **moon**.
2. Spin the **hours** and **min** wheels to the time you want, then click **Start**.
3. In the last minute the volume fades down. When time's up, your video or music
   pauses and the Mac goes to sleep. The volume goes back to normal when you wake it.

Tip: click the **gear** icon and turn on **Open when Mac starts** so the moon is always there.

## Why the warning?

Apple shows this for any app whose developer hasn't paid for Apple's yearly
developer programme. It doesn't mean the app is harmful. All of Sleep Timer's code
is public on GitHub, so anyone can check exactly what it does.

## Removing it

Click the moon → gear icon → **Quit Sleep Timer**, then drag **Sleep Timer** from
Applications to the Bin.
